The route from Westerham to Gatwick

From Westerham, your driver normally takes the A25 west to Junction 6 of the M25 at Godstone, then follows the M25 clockwise to Junction 7 and takes the M23 south to Gatwick at Junction 9.

The stretch most likely to slow you down is the M25 between Junctions 5 and 7, which can queue at weekday peaks and after incidents, so we build in a buffer for morning departures.

In normal traffic the 17-mile journey from Westerham to Gatwick Airport takes 30–40 mins. The planning table below assumes you want to reach the airport two hours before a short-haul flight or three hours before long-haul, uses the upper end of the journey time and adds a 30-minute buffer.

Pickup in Westerham

Westerham is a small historic town on the A25 between Sevenoaks and Oxted, with the M25 just to the north. It has no station of its own, which makes a pre-booked taxi the easiest way to the airports.

We collect from TN16, including the Green, Quebec Square, the roads towards Chartwell, Brasted, Crockham Hill and Biggin Hill. Give us your full postcode when you book and your driver comes to your door.

At Gatwick Airport

Gatwick has two terminals, North and South, linked by a free shuttle train. Your airline booking shows which one you need, and your driver goes straight to the right forecourt.

Gatwick charges for using its terminal drop-off zones (£10 for up to 10 minutes at the time of writing). The fee is set by the airport, not by us, and can change, so ask us at booking how it is handled on your fare.

Most airlines recommend arriving two hours before a short-haul flight and three hours before long-haul.

Gatwick to Westerham taxi

Flying back into Gatwick Airport? We run the same fixed-price service from Gatwick to any address in Westerham. For arrivals, your driver tracks your flight and meets you inside the terminal, so a late landing does not change your fare. The first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free, and you can book the return leg at the same time as your outbound journey.

Westerham to Gatwick taxi: frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Westerham to Gatwick Airport?

A fixed-price taxi from Westerham to Gatwick Airport costs from £110 for a saloon carrying one to four passengers. An estate or MPV is about £132, a minibus about £154 and an executive saloon about £165. The fare is agreed when you book and does not change with traffic or flight delays.

How long does a taxi from Westerham to Gatwick take?

The journey is about 17 miles by road and normally takes 30–40 mins. Allow more time at weekday peaks (roughly 7–9:30am and 4–7pm), on Friday afternoons and at the start of school holidays.

Which route do you take from Westerham to Gatwick?

From Westerham, your driver normally takes the A25 west to Junction 6 of the M25 at Godstone, then follows the M25 clockwise to Junction 7 and takes the M23 south to Gatwick at Junction 9. Your driver checks live traffic before setting off and can change route if there is an incident.

What time should I be picked up in Westerham for my flight?

Plan to be at Gatwick two hours before a short-haul flight or three hours before long-haul. Add the 30–40 mins journey and a 30-minute buffer. For a 9am short-haul departure, that means a pickup around 05:50. We confirm the right pickup time when you book.

Do you charge extra if my flight to or from Gatwick is delayed?

No. We track your flight and adjust the pickup automatically, so a delay never changes your fixed fare. For arrivals, the first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free.

Where will my driver meet me at Gatwick Airport?

For arrivals, your driver tracks your flight and meets you inside the terminal, so a late landing does not change your fare. Your driver waits with a name board and helps with your luggage to the car.

Are there any extra charges on the way to Gatwick?

Gatwick charges for using its terminal drop-off zones (£10 for up to 10 minutes at the time of writing). The fee is set by the airport, not by us, and can change, so ask us at booking how it is handled on your fare.

Can you collect me from home or from Oxted station (nearest) in Westerham?

Yes. We collect from any address in TN16, or from Oxted station (nearest) or a landmark you name when booking. Give us the full postcode so your driver can find rural addresses easily.

Can you take me back from Gatwick to Westerham?

Yes. We run the same fixed-price service from Gatwick Airport back to any address in Westerham, with flight tracking and meet & greet inside the terminal. Book both legs together and we will confirm both fares.

How many passengers and cases fit?

A saloon takes up to four passengers with standard luggage, an estate or MPV five or six, and a minibus seven or eight. Tell us how many large cases you have so we send a vehicle with enough room.

Do you run early-morning taxis from Westerham?

Yes, we operate 24 hours a day, seven days a week. For pickups before 6am please book at least 48 hours ahead so your driver is confirmed well before the day.

Are your drivers licensed?

Yes. All our drivers are licensed by Tunbridge Wells Borough Council, DBS-checked and professionally trained, and we are a licensed private hire operator in Kent.
