The route from Sevenoaks to Harwich

From Sevenoaks, your driver normally joins the M25 at Junction 5 on the edge of town, then follows the M25 anticlockwise over the Dartford Crossing to Junction 28, takes the A12 north-east to Colchester and the A120 east to Harwich International at Parkeston Quay.

The Dartford Crossing is the main pinch-point: northbound queues can build quickly at peak times and after incidents, and the crossing has a road-user charge (Dart Charge). Ask us how the charge is handled on your fixed fare.

In normal traffic the 88-mile journey from Sevenoaks to Harwich International Port takes 1 hr 50 – 2 hr 25.

Pickup in Sevenoaks

Sevenoaks sits right beside Junction 5 of the M25, so it has one of the shortest motorway approaches of any town we serve. That makes a real difference on early departures, when the ring road is at its quietest.

We collect from TN13, TN14 and TN15, including the High Street, the roads around Sevenoaks and Bat & Ball stations, Riverhead, Dunton Green, Kippington, Sevenoaks Weald and the edge of Knole Park. Give us your full postcode and your driver comes to your door.

At Harwich International Port

Harwich International at Parkeston Quay handles Stena Line ferries to Hook of Holland and visiting cruise ships. Tell us whether you are sailing by ferry or cruise so your driver uses the right terminal.

Your driver takes you to the passenger terminal. The route uses the Dartford Crossing, which has a road-user charge, so ask us at booking how it is handled on your fare.

Use your ferry or cruise operator's check-in time to set the pickup time; we add a buffer for the Dartford Crossing.

Harwich to Sevenoaks taxi

Coming home from Harwich? We collect you from Harwich International Port and drive you back to your door in Sevenoaks. For return journeys, tell us your sailing, and your driver collects you from the terminal. You can book the return leg at the same time as your outbound journey.

Sevenoaks to Harwich taxi: frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Sevenoaks to Harwich International Port?

A fixed-price taxi from Sevenoaks to Harwich International Port costs from £203 for a saloon carrying one to four passengers. An estate or MPV is about £244, a minibus about £284 and an executive saloon about £305. The fare is agreed when you book and does not change with traffic.

How long does a taxi from Sevenoaks to Harwich take?

The journey is about 88 miles by road and normally takes 1 hr 50 – 2 hr 25. Allow more time at weekday peaks (roughly 7–9:30am and 4–7pm), on Friday afternoons and at the start of school holidays.

Which route do you take from Sevenoaks to Harwich?

From Sevenoaks, your driver normally joins the M25 at Junction 5 on the edge of town, then follows the M25 anticlockwise over the Dartford Crossing to Junction 28, takes the A12 north-east to Colchester and the A120 east to Harwich International at Parkeston Quay. Your driver checks live traffic before setting off and can change route if there is an incident.

When should I leave Sevenoaks for Harwich?

Use your ferry or cruise operator's check-in time to set the pickup time; we add a buffer for the Dartford Crossing. With a journey of 1 hr 50 – 2 hr 25 from Sevenoaks, we will suggest a pickup time when you book.

Will the driver help with luggage at Harwich?

Yes. Luggage help is included, and your driver takes you to the terminal or address you give us rather than a general drop-off point.

Are there any extra charges on the way to Harwich?

Your driver takes you to the passenger terminal. The route uses the Dartford Crossing, which has a road-user charge, so ask us at booking how it is handled on your fare.

Can you collect me from home or from Sevenoaks station in Sevenoaks?

Yes. We collect from any address in TN13, TN14 and TN15, or from Sevenoaks station or a landmark you name when booking. Give us the full postcode so your driver can find rural addresses easily.

Can you take me back from Harwich to Sevenoaks?

Yes. We run the same fixed-price service from Harwich International Port back to any address in Sevenoaks. Book both legs together and we will confirm both fares.

How many passengers and cases fit?

A saloon takes up to four passengers with standard luggage, an estate or MPV five or six, and a minibus seven or eight. Tell us how many large cases you have so we send a vehicle with enough room.

Do you run early-morning taxis from Sevenoaks?

Yes, we operate 24 hours a day, seven days a week. For pickups before 6am please book at least 48 hours ahead so your driver is confirmed well before the day.

Are your drivers licensed?

Yes. All our drivers are licensed by Tunbridge Wells Borough Council, DBS-checked and professionally trained, and we are a licensed private hire operator in Kent.
