The route from Maidstone to Heathrow

From Maidstone, your driver normally takes the M20 west and the M26 to Junction 5 of the M25, then follows the M25 clockwise past the M23 and M3 junctions to Junction 14 for Terminals 4 and 5, or Junction 15 and the M4 spur for Terminals 2 and 3.

The west side of the M25, between the M23 and the Heathrow junctions, is one of the busiest roads in the country, so morning and Friday departures are planned with extra margin.

In normal traffic the 58-mile journey from Maidstone to Heathrow Airport takes 1 hr 15 – 1 hr 40. The planning table below assumes you want to reach the airport two hours before a short-haul flight or three hours before long-haul, uses the upper end of the journey time and adds a 30-minute buffer.

Pickup in Maidstone

Maidstone is Kent's county town, and its position on the M20 makes it quick to reach both the Channel ports to the east and, via the M26 and M25, the London airports to the west.

We collect from ME14, ME15, ME16 and ME17, including the town centre around Week Street and Fremlin Walk, the streets near Maidstone East and West stations, Allington, Bearsted, Loose and Coxheath. Tell us your postcode and we come to your door.

At Heathrow Airport

Heathrow has four passenger terminals: 2, 3, 4 and 5 (Terminal 1 closed in 2015). Terminals 2 and 3 share the central area, while 4 and 5 have their own approach roads, so tell us your terminal when you book.

Heathrow charges a terminal drop-off fee (£7 at the time of writing, with a 10-minute limit). The fee is set by the airport, not by us, and can change, so ask us at booking how it is handled on your fare.

Most airlines recommend arriving two hours before a short-haul flight and three hours before long-haul.

Heathrow to Maidstone taxi

Flying back into Heathrow Airport? We run the same fixed-price service from Heathrow to any address in Maidstone. For arrivals, your driver tracks your flight and meets you inside the terminal, so a late landing or long immigration queue does not change your fare. The first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free, and you can book the return leg at the same time as your outbound journey.

Maidstone to Heathrow taxi: frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Maidstone to Heathrow Airport?

A fixed-price taxi from Maidstone to Heathrow Airport costs from £137 for a saloon carrying one to four passengers. An estate or MPV is about £164, a minibus about £192 and an executive saloon about £206. The fare is agreed when you book and does not change with traffic or flight delays.

How long does a taxi from Maidstone to Heathrow take?

The journey is about 58 miles by road and normally takes 1 hr 15 – 1 hr 40. Allow more time at weekday peaks (roughly 7–9:30am and 4–7pm), on Friday afternoons and at the start of school holidays.

Which route do you take from Maidstone to Heathrow?

From Maidstone, your driver normally takes the M20 west and the M26 to Junction 5 of the M25, then follows the M25 clockwise past the M23 and M3 junctions to Junction 14 for Terminals 4 and 5, or Junction 15 and the M4 spur for Terminals 2 and 3. Your driver checks live traffic before setting off and can change route if there is an incident.

What time should I be picked up in Maidstone for my flight?

Plan to be at Heathrow two hours before a short-haul flight or three hours before long-haul. Add the 1 hr 15 – 1 hr 40 journey and a 30-minute buffer. For a 9am short-haul departure, that means a pickup around 04:50. We confirm the right pickup time when you book.

Do you charge extra if my flight to or from Heathrow is delayed?

No. We track your flight and adjust the pickup automatically, so a delay never changes your fixed fare. For arrivals, the first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free.

Where will my driver meet me at Heathrow Airport?

For arrivals, your driver tracks your flight and meets you inside the terminal, so a late landing or long immigration queue does not change your fare. Your driver waits with a name board and helps with your luggage to the car.

Are there any extra charges on the way to Heathrow?

Heathrow charges a terminal drop-off fee (£7 at the time of writing, with a 10-minute limit). The fee is set by the airport, not by us, and can change, so ask us at booking how it is handled on your fare.

Can you collect me from home or from Maidstone East station in Maidstone?

Yes. We collect from any address in ME14, ME15, ME16 and ME17, or from Maidstone East station or a landmark you name when booking. Give us the full postcode so your driver can find rural addresses easily.

Can you take me back from Heathrow to Maidstone?

Yes. We run the same fixed-price service from Heathrow Airport back to any address in Maidstone, with flight tracking and meet & greet inside the terminal. Book both legs together and we will confirm both fares.

How many passengers and cases fit?

A saloon takes up to four passengers with standard luggage, an estate or MPV five or six, and a minibus seven or eight. Tell us how many large cases you have so we send a vehicle with enough room.

Do you run early-morning taxis from Maidstone?

Yes, we operate 24 hours a day, seven days a week. For pickups before 6am please book at least 48 hours ahead so your driver is confirmed well before the day.

Are your drivers licensed?

Yes. All our drivers are licensed by Tunbridge Wells Borough Council, DBS-checked and professionally trained, and we are a licensed private hire operator in Kent.
