The route from Tunbridge Wells to Southampton

From Tunbridge Wells, your driver normally heads north on the A21 past Tonbridge to Junction 5 of the M25 near Sevenoaks, then follows the M25 clockwise to Junction 12 and takes the M3 south-west to Junction 14, where the A33 runs down into the city and the docks.

The M25 between Junctions 5 and 12 is the main variable, and the M3 can be busy on summer Saturdays when most cruises turn around, so we plan with a generous buffer.

In normal traffic the 96-mile journey from Tunbridge Wells to Southampton Cruise Terminals takes 2 hr – 2 hr 35.

Pickup in Tunbridge Wells

Royal Tunbridge Wells is the main town in this corner of Kent and the most common starting point for the airport runs we make, from town-centre flats near the Pantiles to family homes in Pembury, Rusthall and Langton Green.

We cover TN1, TN2, TN3 and TN4, including the Pantiles, Mount Pleasant, Calverley Road, St John's, Sherwood, Pembury, Rusthall, Langton Green and the roads around Tunbridge Wells and High Brooms stations. Give us your full address and your driver collects you from the door.

At Southampton Cruise Terminals

Southampton has several cruise terminals across the Eastern and Western Docks, including Ocean, Mayflower, City, QEII and Horizon. Your cruise documents name your terminal, and your driver goes straight to it.

Your driver takes you to the terminal's passenger drop-off, where porters take your cases. Port arrangements can change, so give us your ship and terminal when you book.

Cruise lines issue boarding windows; use yours to set the pickup time, and we add a buffer for the M25 and M3.

Southampton to Tunbridge Wells taxi

For disembarkation, tell us your ship and terminal, and your driver collects you after you clear the terminal. You can book the return leg at the same time as your outbound journey.

Tunbridge Wells to Southampton taxi: frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Tunbridge Wells to Southampton Cruise Terminals?

A fixed-price taxi from Tunbridge Wells to Southampton Cruise Terminals costs from £126 for a saloon carrying one to four passengers. An estate or MPV is about £151, a minibus about £176 and an executive saloon about £189. The fare is agreed when you book and does not change with traffic.

How long does a taxi from Tunbridge Wells to Southampton take?

The journey is about 96 miles by road and normally takes 2 hr – 2 hr 35. Allow more time at weekday peaks (roughly 7–9:30am and 4–7pm), on Friday afternoons and at the start of school holidays.

Which route do you take from Tunbridge Wells to Southampton?

From Tunbridge Wells, your driver normally heads north on the A21 past Tonbridge to Junction 5 of the M25 near Sevenoaks, then follows the M25 clockwise to Junction 12 and takes the M3 south-west to Junction 14, where the A33 runs down into the city and the docks. Your driver checks live traffic before setting off and can change route if there is an incident.

When should I leave Tunbridge Wells for Southampton?

Cruise lines issue boarding windows; use yours to set the pickup time, and we add a buffer for the M25 and M3. With a journey of 2 hr – 2 hr 35 from Tunbridge Wells, we will suggest a pickup time when you book.

Will the driver help with luggage at Southampton?

Yes. Luggage help is included, and your driver takes you to the terminal or address you give us rather than a general drop-off point.

Are there any extra charges on the way to Southampton?

Your driver takes you to the terminal's passenger drop-off, where porters take your cases. Port arrangements can change, so give us your ship and terminal when you book.

Can you collect me from home or from Tunbridge Wells station in Tunbridge Wells?

Yes. We collect from any address in TN1, TN2, TN3 and TN4, or from Tunbridge Wells station or a landmark you name when booking. Give us the full postcode so your driver can find rural addresses easily.

Can you take me back from Southampton to Tunbridge Wells?

Yes. We run the same fixed-price service from Southampton Cruise Terminals back to any address in Tunbridge Wells. Book both legs together and we will confirm both fares.

How many passengers and cases fit?

A saloon takes up to four passengers with standard luggage, an estate or MPV five or six, and a minibus seven or eight. Tell us how many large cases you have so we send a vehicle with enough room.

Do you run early-morning taxis from Tunbridge Wells?

Yes, we operate 24 hours a day, seven days a week. For pickups before 6am please book at least 48 hours ahead so your driver is confirmed well before the day.

Are your drivers licensed?

Yes. All our drivers are licensed by Tunbridge Wells Borough Council, DBS-checked and professionally trained, and we are a licensed private hire operator in Kent.
