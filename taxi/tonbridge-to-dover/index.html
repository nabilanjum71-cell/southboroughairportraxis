The route from Tonbridge to Dover

From Tonbridge, your driver normally takes the A26 east through Hadlow and the A228 to join the M20 near West Malling, then follows the M20 east to its end beyond Folkestone and takes the A20 along the cliffs into Dover and the Eastern Docks.

The M20 is usually free-flowing east of Maidstone, but cross-Channel traffic management (Operation Brock) can be in place at busy times, particularly in summer and around holidays. Your driver checks the latest before setting off.

In normal traffic the 60-mile journey from Tonbridge to Port of Dover takes 1 hr 15 – 1 hr 40.

Pickup in Tonbridge

Tonbridge sits right beside the A21, which gives it a quick, simple run to the M25 and the western airports, and a short hop east to the M20 for the Channel ports.

We collect from TN9, TN10 and TN11, including the High Street, the castle and Tonbridge School area, Higham Wood, Hildenborough, Hadlow and the roads around Tonbridge station. Give us your full postcode when you book and your driver comes to your door.

At Port of Dover

The Port of Dover handles cross-Channel ferries at the Eastern Docks and cruise ships at its cruise terminals on the Western Docks. Ferry operators set their own rules on foot passengers, so check with your operator, and tell us which terminal you need.

Your driver takes you to the correct terminal building for your sailing. Port access arrangements can change, so tell us your operator and sailing time when you book.

Cruise lines issue boarding windows, and ferry operators publish check-in times; use those to set your pickup time, and we will add a traffic buffer.

Dover to Tonbridge taxi

Coming home from Dover? We collect you from Port of Dover and drive you back to your door in Tonbridge. For returning cruise and ferry passengers, we agree a collection time and your driver waits for you at the terminal. You can book the return leg at the same time as your outbound journey.

Tonbridge to Dover taxi: frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Tonbridge to Port of Dover?

A fixed-price taxi from Tonbridge to Port of Dover costs from £90 for a saloon carrying one to four passengers. An estate or MPV is about £108, a minibus about £126 and an executive saloon about £135. The fare is agreed when you book and does not change with traffic.

How long does a taxi from Tonbridge to Dover take?

The journey is about 60 miles by road and normally takes 1 hr 15 – 1 hr 40. Allow more time at weekday peaks (roughly 7–9:30am and 4–7pm), on Friday afternoons and at the start of school holidays.

Which route do you take from Tonbridge to Dover?

From Tonbridge, your driver normally takes the A26 east through Hadlow and the A228 to join the M20 near West Malling, then follows the M20 east to its end beyond Folkestone and takes the A20 along the cliffs into Dover and the Eastern Docks. Your driver checks live traffic before setting off and can change route if there is an incident.

When should I leave Tonbridge for Dover?

Cruise lines issue boarding windows, and ferry operators publish check-in times; use those to set your pickup time, and we will add a traffic buffer. With a journey of 1 hr 15 – 1 hr 40 from Tonbridge, we will suggest a pickup time when you book.

Will the driver help with luggage at Dover?

Yes. Luggage help is included, and your driver takes you to the terminal or address you give us rather than a general drop-off point.

Are there any extra charges on the way to Dover?

Your driver takes you to the correct terminal building for your sailing. Port access arrangements can change, so tell us your operator and sailing time when you book.

Can you collect me from home or from Tonbridge station in Tonbridge?

Yes. We collect from any address in TN9, TN10 and TN11, or from Tonbridge station or a landmark you name when booking. Give us the full postcode so your driver can find rural addresses easily.

Can you take me back from Dover to Tonbridge?

Yes. We run the same fixed-price service from Port of Dover back to any address in Tonbridge. Book both legs together and we will confirm both fares.

How many passengers and cases fit?

A saloon takes up to four passengers with standard luggage, an estate or MPV five or six, and a minibus seven or eight. Tell us how many large cases you have so we send a vehicle with enough room.

Do you run early-morning taxis from Tonbridge?

Yes, we operate 24 hours a day, seven days a week. For pickups before 6am please book at least 48 hours ahead so your driver is confirmed well before the day.

Are your drivers licensed?

Yes. All our drivers are licensed by Tunbridge Wells Borough Council, DBS-checked and professionally trained, and we are a licensed private hire operator in Kent.
