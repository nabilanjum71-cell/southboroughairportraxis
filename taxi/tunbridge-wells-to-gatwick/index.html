The route from Tunbridge Wells to Gatwick

From Tunbridge Wells, your driver normally heads north on the A21 past Tonbridge to Junction 5 of the M25 near Sevenoaks, then follows the M25 clockwise to Junction 7 and takes the M23 south to Gatwick at Junction 9. When the M25 is congested, the A264 west through Langton Green, Groombridge and East Grinstead to the M23 at Junction 10 is the usual alternative.

The stretch most likely to slow you down is the M25 between Junctions 5 and 7, which can queue at weekday peaks and after incidents, so we build in a buffer for morning departures.

Pickup in Tunbridge Wells

Royal Tunbridge Wells is the main town in this corner of Kent and the most common starting point for the airport runs we make, from town-centre flats near the Pantiles to family homes in Pembury, Rusthall and Langton Green.

We cover TN1, TN2, TN3 and TN4, including the Pantiles, Mount Pleasant, Calverley Road, St John's, Sherwood, Pembury, Rusthall, Langton Green and the roads around Tunbridge Wells and High Brooms stations. Give us your full address and your driver collects you from the door.

At Gatwick Airport

Gatwick has two terminals, North and South, linked by a free shuttle train. Your airline booking shows which one you need, and your driver goes straight to the right forecourt.

Gatwick charges for using its terminal drop-off zones (£10 for up to 10 minutes at the time of writing). The fee is set by the airport, not by us, and can change, so ask us at booking how it is handled on your fare.

Most airlines recommend arriving two hours before a short-haul flight and three hours before long-haul.

Gatwick to Tunbridge Wells taxi

Flying back into Gatwick Airport? We run the same fixed-price service from Gatwick to any address in Tunbridge Wells. For arrivals, your driver tracks your flight and meets you inside the terminal, so a late landing does not change your fare. The first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free, and you can book the return leg at the same time as your outbound journey.

Tunbridge Wells to Gatwick taxi: frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Tunbridge Wells to Gatwick Airport?

A fixed-price taxi from Tunbridge Wells to Gatwick Airport costs from £78 for a saloon carrying one to four passengers. An estate or MPV is about £94, a minibus about £109 and an executive saloon about £117. The fare is agreed when you book and does not change with traffic or flight delays.

How long does a taxi from Tunbridge Wells to Gatwick take?

The journey is about 25 miles by road and normally takes 40–55 mins. Allow more time at weekday peaks (roughly 7–9:30am and 4–7pm), on Friday afternoons and at the start of school holidays.

Which route do you take from Tunbridge Wells to Gatwick?

From Tunbridge Wells, your driver normally heads north on the A21 past Tonbridge to Junction 5 of the M25 near Sevenoaks, then follows the M25 clockwise to Junction 7 and takes the M23 south to Gatwick at Junction 9. When the M25 is congested, the A264 west through Langton Green, Groombridge and East Grinstead to the M23 at Junction 10 is the usual alternative. Your driver checks live traffic before setting off and can change route if there is an incident.

What time should I be picked up in Tunbridge Wells for my flight?

Plan to be at Gatwick two hours before a short-haul flight or three hours before long-haul. Add the 40–55 mins journey and a 30-minute buffer. For a 9am short-haul departure, that means a pickup around 05:35. We confirm the right pickup time when you book.

Do you charge extra if my flight to or from Gatwick is delayed?

No. We track your flight and adjust the pickup automatically, so a delay never changes your fixed fare. For arrivals, the first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free.

Where will my driver meet me at Gatwick Airport?

For arrivals, your driver tracks your flight and meets you inside the terminal, so a late landing does not change your fare. Your driver waits with a name board and helps with your luggage to the car.

Are there any extra charges on the way to Gatwick?

Gatwick charges for using its terminal drop-off zones (£10 for up to 10 minutes at the time of writing). The fee is set by the airport, not by us, and can change, so ask us at booking how it is handled on your fare.

Can you collect me from home or from Tunbridge Wells station in Tunbridge Wells?

Yes. We collect from any address in TN1, TN2, TN3 and TN4, or from Tunbridge Wells station or a landmark you name when booking. Give us the full postcode so your driver can find rural addresses easily.

Can you take me back from Gatwick to Tunbridge Wells?

Yes. We run the same fixed-price service from Gatwick Airport back to any address in Tunbridge Wells, with flight tracking and meet & greet inside the terminal. Book both legs together and we will confirm both fares.

How many passengers and cases fit?

A saloon takes up to four passengers with standard luggage, an estate or MPV five or six, and a minibus seven or eight. Tell us how many large cases you have so we send a vehicle with enough room.

Do you run early-morning taxis from Tunbridge Wells?

Yes, we operate 24 hours a day, seven days a week. For pickups before 6am please book at least 48 hours ahead so your driver is confirmed well before the day.

Are your drivers licensed?

Yes. All our drivers are licensed by Tunbridge Wells Borough Council, DBS-checked and professionally trained, and we are a licensed private hire operator in Kent.
