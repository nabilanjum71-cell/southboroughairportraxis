The route from Southborough to London City

From Southborough, your driver normally takes the A26 north towards Tonbridge and joins the A21, following it north to Junction 5 of the M25 near Sevenoaks, then follows the M25 anticlockwise to Junction 2, takes the A2 towards London and crosses under the Thames by the Blackwall or Silvertown tunnel to the Royal Docks.

The Blackwall Tunnel approach is often congested at peak times; the Silvertown Tunnel, which opened in 2025, gives an alternative. Both tunnels carry a user charge, so ask us how it is handled on your fare.

In normal traffic the 34-mile journey from Southborough to London City Airport takes 45–65 mins. The planning table below assumes you want to reach the airport two hours before a short-haul flight or three hours before long-haul, uses the upper end of the journey time and adds a 30-minute buffer.

Pickup in Southborough

Southborough sits between Royal Tunbridge Wells and Tonbridge on the A26, and it is where we are based. Pickups here are the shortest positioning runs we make, so a driver is rarely far away, even for a 4am departure.

We collect from every TN4 address, including London Road, Modest Corner, Southborough Common, Pennington Road, St John's Road and the streets around High Brooms station. Give us your full postcode and your driver comes to your door rather than a fixed meeting point.

At London City Airport

London City has a single, compact terminal in the Royal Docks, so there is nothing to choose and the walk from the drop-off to check-in is short.

London City manages its own forecourt and drop-off rules, which can change. The Blackwall and Silvertown tunnels on the usual route carry a user charge, so ask us at booking how tolls and airport fees are handled on your fare.

London City is known for short processing times, but most airlines still ask you to be at the airport at least an hour before departure, longer if you have hold luggage.

London City to Southborough taxi

Flying back into London City Airport? We run the same fixed-price service from London City to any address in Southborough. For arrivals, your driver tracks your flight and meets you in the arrivals area, so a late landing does not change your fare. The first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free, and you can book the return leg at the same time as your outbound journey.

Southborough to London City taxi: frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Southborough to London City Airport?

A fixed-price taxi from Southborough to London City Airport costs from £90 for a saloon carrying one to four passengers. An estate or MPV is about £108, a minibus about £126 and an executive saloon about £135. The fare is agreed when you book and does not change with traffic or flight delays.

How long does a taxi from Southborough to London City take?

The journey is about 34 miles by road and normally takes 45–65 mins. Allow more time at weekday peaks (roughly 7–9:30am and 4–7pm), on Friday afternoons and at the start of school holidays.

Which route do you take from Southborough to London City?

From Southborough, your driver normally takes the A26 north towards Tonbridge and joins the A21, following it north to Junction 5 of the M25 near Sevenoaks, then follows the M25 anticlockwise to Junction 2, takes the A2 towards London and crosses under the Thames by the Blackwall or Silvertown tunnel to the Royal Docks. Your driver checks live traffic before setting off and can change route if there is an incident.

What time should I be picked up in Southborough for my flight?

Plan to be at London City two hours before a short-haul flight or three hours before long-haul. Add the 45–65 mins journey and a 30-minute buffer. For a 9am short-haul departure, that means a pickup around 05:25. We confirm the right pickup time when you book.

Do you charge extra if my flight to or from London City is delayed?

No. We track your flight and adjust the pickup automatically, so a delay never changes your fixed fare. For arrivals, the first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free.

Where will my driver meet me at London City Airport?

For arrivals, your driver tracks your flight and meets you in the arrivals area, so a late landing does not change your fare. Your driver waits with a name board and helps with your luggage to the car.

Are there any extra charges on the way to London City?

London City manages its own forecourt and drop-off rules, which can change. The Blackwall and Silvertown tunnels on the usual route carry a user charge, so ask us at booking how tolls and airport fees are handled on your fare.

Can you collect me from home or from High Brooms station in Southborough?

Yes. We collect from any address in TN4, or from High Brooms station or a landmark you name when booking. Give us the full postcode so your driver can find rural addresses easily.

Can you take me back from London City to Southborough?

Yes. We run the same fixed-price service from London City Airport back to any address in Southborough, with flight tracking and meet & greet inside the terminal. Book both legs together and we will confirm both fares.

How many passengers and cases fit?

A saloon takes up to four passengers with standard luggage, an estate or MPV five or six, and a minibus seven or eight. Tell us how many large cases you have so we send a vehicle with enough room.

Do you run early-morning taxis from Southborough?

Yes, we operate 24 hours a day, seven days a week. For pickups before 6am please book at least 48 hours ahead so your driver is confirmed well before the day.

Are your drivers licensed?

Yes. All our drivers are licensed by Tunbridge Wells Borough Council, DBS-checked and professionally trained, and we are a licensed private hire operator in Kent.
