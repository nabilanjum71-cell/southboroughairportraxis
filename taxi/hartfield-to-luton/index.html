The route from Hartfield to Luton

From Hartfield, your driver normally heads to Forest Row and takes the A22 north through East Grinstead to Junction 6 of the M25, then follows the M25 clockwise round the west of London to Junction 21 and takes the M1 north to Junction 10 for the airport.

The west and north-west sides of the M25 up to the M1 are the busiest part of the journey, so we plan extra time for weekday morning and Friday departures.

In normal traffic the 74-mile journey from Hartfield to Luton Airport takes 1 hr 35 – 2 hr 5. The planning table below assumes you want to reach the airport two hours before a short-haul flight or three hours before long-haul, uses the upper end of the journey time and adds a 30-minute buffer.

Pickup in Hartfield

Hartfield is a village on the northern edge of Ashdown Forest in East Sussex, with no railway station. A pre-booked taxi is the practical way to an airport or port, especially with luggage or an early start.

We collect from TN7, including the High Street, Coleman's Hatch, Withyham, Forest Row and the lanes across Ashdown Forest. Give us your full postcode, and any directions for rural addresses, when you book.

At Luton Airport

Luton has a single passenger terminal. Your driver uses the terminal drop-off area and helps with your luggage.

Luton charges for using its terminal drop-off zone. The fee is set by the airport and can change, so ask us at booking how it is handled on your fare.

Most airlines recommend arriving two hours before a short-haul flight.

Luton to Hartfield taxi

Flying back into Luton Airport? We run the same fixed-price service from Luton to any address in Hartfield. Hartfield to Luton taxi: frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Hartfield to Luton Airport?

A fixed-price taxi from Hartfield to Luton Airport costs from £178 for a saloon carrying one to four passengers. An estate or MPV is about £214, a minibus about £249 and an executive saloon about £267. The fare is agreed when you book and does not change with traffic or flight delays.

How long does a taxi from Hartfield to Luton take?

The journey is about 74 miles by road and normally takes 1 hr 35 – 2 hr 5. Allow more time at weekday peaks (roughly 7–9:30am and 4–7pm), on Friday afternoons and at the start of school holidays.

Which route do you take from Hartfield to Luton?

From Hartfield, your driver normally heads to Forest Row and takes the A22 north through East Grinstead to Junction 6 of the M25, then follows the M25 clockwise round the west of London to Junction 21 and takes the M1 north to Junction 10 for the airport. Your driver checks live traffic before setting off and can change route if there is an incident.

What time should I be picked up in Hartfield for my flight?

Plan to be at Luton two hours before a short-haul flight or three hours before long-haul. Add the 1 hr 35 – 2 hr 5 journey and a 30-minute buffer. For a 9am short-haul departure, that means a pickup around 04:25. We confirm the right pickup time when you book.

Do you charge extra if my flight to or from Luton is delayed?

No. We track your flight and adjust the pickup automatically, so a delay never changes your fixed fare. For arrivals, the first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free.

Where will my driver meet me at Luton Airport?

For arrivals, your driver tracks your flight and meets you inside the terminal, so a late landing does not change your fare. Your driver waits with a name board and helps with your luggage to the car.

Are there any extra charges on the way to Luton?

Luton charges for using its terminal drop-off zone. The fee is set by the airport and can change, so ask us at booking how it is handled on your fare.

Can you collect me from home or from East Grinstead station (nearest) in Hartfield?

Yes. We collect from any address in TN7, or from East Grinstead station (nearest) or a landmark you name when booking. Give us the full postcode so your driver can find rural addresses easily.

Can you take me back from Luton to Hartfield?

Yes. We run the same fixed-price service from Luton Airport back to any address in Hartfield, with flight tracking and meet & greet inside the terminal. Book both legs together and we will confirm both fares.

How many passengers and cases fit?

A saloon takes up to four passengers with standard luggage, an estate or MPV five or six, and a minibus seven or eight. Tell us how many large cases you have so we send a vehicle with enough room.

Do you run early-morning taxis from Hartfield?

Yes, we operate 24 hours a day, seven days a week. For pickups before 6am please book at least 48 hours ahead so your driver is confirmed well before the day.

Are your drivers licensed?

Yes. All our drivers are licensed by Tunbridge Wells Borough Council, DBS-checked and professionally trained, and we are a licensed private hire operator in Kent.
