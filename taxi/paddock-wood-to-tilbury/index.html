The route from Paddock Wood to Tilbury

From Paddock Wood, your driver normally heads west on the B2017 into Tonbridge and joins the A21 north to Junction 5 of the M25, then follows the M25 anticlockwise over the Dartford Crossing, leaves at Junction 30 for the A13 east and follows the A1089 down to Tilbury and the cruise terminal.

The Dartford Crossing is the main pinch-point: northbound queues can build quickly at peak times and after incidents, and the crossing has a road-user charge (Dart Charge). Ask us how the charge is handled on your fixed fare.

In normal traffic the 26-mile journey from Paddock Wood to London Tilbury Cruise Terminal takes 35–50 mins.

Pickup in Paddock Wood

Paddock Wood is a small Weald town surrounded by orchards and old hop country. It is a little further from the motorways than Tonbridge, so a fixed door-to-door fare removes the hassle of getting to a bigger station first.

We collect from TN12, including Commercial Road, the roads around Paddock Wood station and the surrounding villages of Five Oak Green, Brenchley, Matfield, Horsmonden and East Peckham. Give us your full postcode so your driver can find rural addresses without delay.

At London Tilbury Cruise Terminal

Tilbury's London International Cruise Terminal sits on the Thames riverside and is used by several cruise lines, including Ambassador Cruise Line. Check your cruise documents for your boarding window.

Your driver takes you to the cruise terminal drop-off and helps with luggage. The route uses the Dartford Crossing, which has a road-user charge, so ask us how it is handled on your fare.

Cruise lines allocate boarding times; arrive within your window rather than early, and we will plan the pickup around it.

Tilbury to Paddock Wood taxi

Coming home from Tilbury? We collect you from London Tilbury Cruise Terminal and drive you back to your door in Paddock Wood. For disembarkation, tell us your ship and expected arrival, and your driver collects you from the terminal. You can book the return leg at the same time as your outbound journey.

Paddock Wood to Tilbury taxi: frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Paddock Wood to London Tilbury Cruise Terminal?

A fixed-price taxi from Paddock Wood to London Tilbury Cruise Terminal costs from £116 for a saloon carrying one to four passengers. An estate or MPV is about £139, a minibus about £162 and an executive saloon about £174. The fare is agreed when you book and does not change with traffic.

How long does a taxi from Paddock Wood to Tilbury take?

The journey is about 26 miles by road and normally takes 35–50 mins. Allow more time at weekday peaks (roughly 7–9:30am and 4–7pm), on Friday afternoons and at the start of school holidays.

Which route do you take from Paddock Wood to Tilbury?

From Paddock Wood, your driver normally heads west on the B2017 into Tonbridge and joins the A21 north to Junction 5 of the M25, then follows the M25 anticlockwise over the Dartford Crossing, leaves at Junction 30 for the A13 east and follows the A1089 down to Tilbury and the cruise terminal. Your driver checks live traffic before setting off and can change route if there is an incident.

When should I leave Paddock Wood for Tilbury?

Cruise lines allocate boarding times; arrive within your window rather than early, and we will plan the pickup around it. With a journey of 35–50 mins from Paddock Wood, we will suggest a pickup time when you book.

Will the driver help with luggage at Tilbury?

Yes. Luggage help is included, and your driver takes you to the terminal or address you give us rather than a general drop-off point.

Are there any extra charges on the way to Tilbury?

Your driver takes you to the cruise terminal drop-off and helps with luggage. The route uses the Dartford Crossing, which has a road-user charge, so ask us how it is handled on your fare.

Can you collect me from home or from Paddock Wood station in Paddock Wood?

Yes. We collect from any address in TN12, or from Paddock Wood station or a landmark you name when booking. Give us the full postcode so your driver can find rural addresses easily.

Can you take me back from Tilbury to Paddock Wood?

Yes. We run the same fixed-price service from London Tilbury Cruise Terminal back to any address in Paddock Wood. Book both legs together and we will confirm both fares.

How many passengers and cases fit?

A saloon takes up to four passengers with standard luggage, an estate or MPV five or six, and a minibus seven or eight. Tell us how many large cases you have so we send a vehicle with enough room.

Do you run early-morning taxis from Paddock Wood?

Yes, we operate 24 hours a day, seven days a week. For pickups before 6am please book at least 48 hours ahead so your driver is confirmed well before the day.

Are your drivers licensed?

Yes. All our drivers are licensed by Tunbridge Wells Borough Council, DBS-checked and professionally trained, and we are a licensed private hire operator in Kent.
