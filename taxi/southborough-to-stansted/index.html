The route from Southborough to Stansted

From Southborough, your driver normally takes the A26 north towards Tonbridge and joins the A21, following it north to Junction 5 of the M25 near Sevenoaks, then follows the M25 anticlockwise over the Dartford Crossing, continues north to Junction 27 and takes the M11 to Junction 8 for the airport.

The Dartford Crossing is the main pinch-point: northbound queues can build quickly at peak times and after incidents, and the crossing has a road-user charge (Dart Charge). Ask us how the charge is handled on your fixed fare.

In normal traffic the 68-mile journey from Southborough to Stansted Airport takes 1 hr 25 – 1 hr 55. Pickup in Southborough

Southborough sits between Royal Tunbridge Wells and Tonbridge on the A26, and it is where we are based. Pickups here are the shortest positioning runs we make, so a driver is rarely far away, even for a 4am departure.

We collect from every TN4 address, including London Road, Modest Corner, Southborough Common, Pennington Road, St John's Road and the streets around High Brooms station. Give us your full postcode and your driver comes to your door rather than a fixed meeting point.

At Stansted Airport

Stansted has a single passenger terminal, so there is no terminal to choose. Your driver drops you at the terminal forecourt and helps with your luggage.

Stansted charges for its terminal drop-off zone (£10 for a short stop at the time of writing). The route also uses the Dartford Crossing, which has a road-user charge. Ask us at booking how both are handled on your fare.

Most airlines recommend arriving two hours before a short-haul flight; Stansted security can be busy at peak times, so allow extra time in school holidays.

Stansted to Southborough taxi

Flying back into Stansted Airport? We run the same fixed-price service from Stansted to any address in Southborough. For arrivals, your driver tracks your flight and meets you inside the terminal, so a late landing does not change your fare. The first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free, and you can book the return leg at the same time as your outbound journey.

Southborough to Stansted taxi: frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Southborough to Stansted Airport?

A fixed-price taxi from Southborough to Stansted Airport costs from £130 for a saloon carrying one to four passengers. An estate or MPV is about £156, a minibus about £182 and an executive saloon about £195. The fare is agreed when you book and does not change with traffic or flight delays.

How long does a taxi from Southborough to Stansted take?

The journey is about 68 miles by road and normally takes 1 hr 25 – 1 hr 55. Allow more time at weekday peaks (roughly 7–9:30am and 4–7pm), on Friday afternoons and at the start of school holidays.

Which route do you take from Southborough to Stansted?

From Southborough, your driver normally takes the A26 north towards Tonbridge and joins the A21, following it north to Junction 5 of the M25 near Sevenoaks, then follows the M25 anticlockwise over the Dartford Crossing, continues north to Junction 27 and takes the M11 to Junction 8 for the airport. Your driver checks live traffic before setting off and can change route if there is an incident.

What time should I be picked up in Southborough for my flight?

Plan to be at Stansted two hours before a short-haul flight or three hours before long-haul. Add the 1 hr 25 – 1 hr 55 journey and a 30-minute buffer. For a 9am short-haul departure, that means a pickup around 04:35. We confirm the right pickup time when you book.

Do you charge extra if my flight to or from Stansted is delayed?

No. We track your flight and adjust the pickup automatically, so a delay never changes your fixed fare. For arrivals, the first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free.

Where will my driver meet me at Stansted Airport?

For arrivals, your driver tracks your flight and meets you inside the terminal, so a late landing does not change your fare. Your driver waits with a name board and helps with your luggage to the car.

Are there any extra charges on the way to Stansted?

Stansted charges for its terminal drop-off zone (£10 for a short stop at the time of writing). The route also uses the Dartford Crossing, which has a road-user charge. Ask us at booking how both are handled on your fare.

Can you collect me from home or from High Brooms station in Southborough?

Yes. We collect from any address in TN4, or from High Brooms station or a landmark you name when booking. Give us the full postcode so your driver can find rural addresses easily.

Can you take me back from Stansted to Southborough?

Yes. We run the same fixed-price service from Stansted Airport back to any address in Southborough, with flight tracking and meet & greet inside the terminal. Book both legs together and we will confirm both fares.

How many passengers and cases fit?

A saloon takes up to four passengers with standard luggage, an estate or MPV five or six, and a minibus seven or eight. Tell us how many large cases you have so we send a vehicle with enough room.

Do you run early-morning taxis from Southborough?

Yes, we operate 24 hours a day, seven days a week. For pickups before 6am please book at least 48 hours ahead so your driver is confirmed well before the day.

Are your drivers licensed?

Yes. All our drivers are licensed by Tunbridge Wells Borough Council, DBS-checked and professionally trained, and we are a licensed private hire operator in Kent.
