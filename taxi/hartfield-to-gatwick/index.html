The route from Hartfield to Gatwick

From Hartfield, your driver normally heads to Forest Row and East Grinstead, then takes the A264 through Copthorne to the M23 at Junction 10 for Gatwick. This cross-country route avoids the M25 altogether. If the A-roads are blocked, the alternative is to join the M25 and approach Gatwick on the M23.

The A22 and A264 are mostly single-carriageway, so roadworks, school traffic and slow vehicles can hold things up, but the distance is short and the route avoids the M25's peak-time queues.

In normal traffic the 16-mile journey from Hartfield to Gatwick Airport takes 25–35 mins. The planning table below assumes you want to reach the airport two hours before a short-haul flight or three hours before long-haul, uses the upper end of the journey time and adds a 30-minute buffer.

Pickup in Hartfield

Hartfield is a village on the northern edge of Ashdown Forest in East Sussex, with no railway station. A pre-booked taxi is the practical way to an airport or port, especially with luggage or an early start.

We collect from TN7, including the High Street, Coleman's Hatch, Withyham, Forest Row and the lanes across Ashdown Forest. Give us your full postcode, and any directions for rural addresses, when you book.

At Gatwick Airport

Gatwick has two terminals, North and South, linked by a free shuttle train. Your airline booking shows which one you need, and your driver goes straight to the right forecourt.

Gatwick charges for using its terminal drop-off zones (£10 for up to 10 minutes at the time of writing). The fee is set by the airport, not by us, and can change, so ask us at booking how it is handled on your fare.

Most airlines recommend arriving two hours before a short-haul flight and three hours before long-haul.

Gatwick to Hartfield taxi

Flying back into Gatwick Airport? We run the same fixed-price service from Gatwick to any address in Hartfield. For arrivals, your driver tracks your flight and meets you inside the terminal, so a late landing does not change your fare. The first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free, and you can book the return leg at the same time as your outbound journey.

Hartfield to Gatwick taxi: frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Hartfield to Gatwick Airport?

A fixed-price taxi from Hartfield to Gatwick Airport costs from £102 for a saloon carrying one to four passengers. An estate or MPV is about £122, a minibus about £143 and an executive saloon about £153. The fare is agreed when you book and does not change with traffic or flight delays.

How long does a taxi from Hartfield to Gatwick take?

The journey is about 16 miles by road and normally takes 25–35 mins. Allow more time at weekday peaks (roughly 7–9:30am and 4–7pm), on Friday afternoons and at the start of school holidays.

Which route do you take from Hartfield to Gatwick?

From Hartfield, your driver normally heads to Forest Row and East Grinstead, then takes the A264 through Copthorne to the M23 at Junction 10 for Gatwick. This cross-country route avoids the M25 altogether. If the A-roads are blocked, the alternative is to join the M25 and approach Gatwick on the M23. Your driver checks live traffic before setting off and can change route if there is an incident.

What time should I be picked up in Hartfield for my flight?

Plan to be at Gatwick two hours before a short-haul flight or three hours before long-haul. Add the 25–35 mins journey and a 30-minute buffer. For a 9am short-haul departure, that means a pickup around 05:55. We confirm the right pickup time when you book.

Do you charge extra if my flight to or from Gatwick is delayed?

No. We track your flight and adjust the pickup automatically, so a delay never changes your fixed fare. For arrivals, the first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free.

Where will my driver meet me at Gatwick Airport?

For arrivals, your driver tracks your flight and meets you inside the terminal, so a late landing does not change your fare. Your driver waits with a name board and helps with your luggage to the car.

Are there any extra charges on the way to Gatwick?

Gatwick charges for using its terminal drop-off zones (£10 for up to 10 minutes at the time of writing). The fee is set by the airport, not by us, and can change, so ask us at booking how it is handled on your fare.

Can you collect me from home or from East Grinstead station (nearest) in Hartfield?

Yes. We collect from any address in TN7, or from East Grinstead station (nearest) or a landmark you name when booking. Give us the full postcode so your driver can find rural addresses easily.

Can you take me back from Gatwick to Hartfield?

Yes. We run the same fixed-price service from Gatwick Airport back to any address in Hartfield, with flight tracking and meet & greet inside the terminal. Book both legs together and we will confirm both fares.

How many passengers and cases fit?

A saloon takes up to four passengers with standard luggage, an estate or MPV five or six, and a minibus seven or eight. Tell us how many large cases you have so we send a vehicle with enough room.

Do you run early-morning taxis from Hartfield?

Yes, we operate 24 hours a day, seven days a week. For pickups before 6am please book at least 48 hours ahead so your driver is confirmed well before the day.

Are your drivers licensed?

Yes. All our drivers are licensed by Tunbridge Wells Borough Council, DBS-checked and professionally trained, and we are a licensed private hire operator in Kent.
