The route from Hartfield to Folkestone

From Hartfield, your driver normally heads east on the A264 to Tunbridge Wells, then north through Tonbridge on the A26 to join the M20 near West Malling, then follows the M20 east past Ashford to Junction 11a for the Eurotunnel terminal at Cheriton, or on to Junctions 12 and 13 for Folkestone town and harbour.

The M20 is usually free-flowing east of Maidstone, but cross-Channel traffic management (Operation Brock) can be in place at busy times, particularly in summer and around holidays. Your driver checks the latest before setting off.

In normal traffic the 58-mile journey from Hartfield to Folkestone & Eurotunnel takes 1 hr 15 – 1 hr 40.

Pickup in Hartfield

Hartfield is a village on the northern edge of Ashdown Forest in East Sussex, with no railway station. A pre-booked taxi is the practical way to an airport or port, especially with luggage or an early start.

We collect from TN7, including the High Street, Coleman's Hatch, Withyham, Forest Row and the lanes across Ashdown Forest. Give us your full postcode, and any directions for rural addresses, when you book.

At Folkestone & Eurotunnel

Folkestone has the Eurotunnel Le Shuttle terminal at Cheriton, Folkestone Central and West stations, and the regenerated Harbour Arm. Le Shuttle carries vehicles rather than foot passengers, so tell us exactly where in Folkestone you are going.

There is no airport-style drop-off fee in Folkestone. Your driver takes you to the exact address, station or terminal building you give us.

If you are meeting a Le Shuttle crossing, check the operator's own check-in window before you choose a pickup time.

Folkestone to Hartfield taxi

For return journeys, we can collect you from any Folkestone address, station or the Eurotunnel terminal at an agreed time. You can book the return leg at the same time as your outbound journey.

Hartfield to Folkestone taxi: frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Hartfield to Folkestone & Eurotunnel?

A fixed-price taxi from Hartfield to Folkestone & Eurotunnel costs from £95 for a saloon carrying one to four passengers. An estate or MPV is about £114, a minibus about £133 and an executive saloon about £143. The fare is agreed when you book and does not change with traffic.

How long does a taxi from Hartfield to Folkestone take?

The journey is about 58 miles by road and normally takes 1 hr 15 – 1 hr 40. Allow more time at weekday peaks (roughly 7–9:30am and 4–7pm), on Friday afternoons and at the start of school holidays.

Which route do you take from Hartfield to Folkestone?

From Hartfield, your driver normally heads east on the A264 to Tunbridge Wells, then north through Tonbridge on the A26 to join the M20 near West Malling, then follows the M20 east past Ashford to Junction 11a for the Eurotunnel terminal at Cheriton, or on to Junctions 12 and 13 for Folkestone town and harbour. Your driver checks live traffic before setting off and can change route if there is an incident.

When should I leave Hartfield for Folkestone?

If you are meeting a Le Shuttle crossing, check the operator's own check-in window before you choose a pickup time. With a journey of 1 hr 15 – 1 hr 40 from Hartfield, we will suggest a pickup time when you book.

Will the driver help with luggage at Folkestone?

Yes. Luggage help is included, and your driver takes you to the terminal or address you give us rather than a general drop-off point.

Are there any extra charges on the way to Folkestone?

There is no airport-style drop-off fee in Folkestone. Your driver takes you to the exact address, station or terminal building you give us.

Can you collect me from home or from East Grinstead station (nearest) in Hartfield?

Yes. We collect from any address in TN7, or from East Grinstead station (nearest) or a landmark you name when booking. Give us the full postcode so your driver can find rural addresses easily.

Can you take me back from Folkestone to Hartfield?

Yes. We run the same fixed-price service from Folkestone & Eurotunnel back to any address in Hartfield. Book both legs together and we will confirm both fares.

How many passengers and cases fit?

A saloon takes up to four passengers with standard luggage, an estate or MPV five or six, and a minibus seven or eight. Tell us how many large cases you have so we send a vehicle with enough room.

Do you run early-morning taxis from Hartfield?

Yes, we operate 24 hours a day, seven days a week. For pickups before 6am please book at least 48 hours ahead so your driver is confirmed well before the day.

Are your drivers licensed?

Yes. All our drivers are licensed by Tunbridge Wells Borough Council, DBS-checked and professionally trained, and we are a licensed private hire operator in Kent.
