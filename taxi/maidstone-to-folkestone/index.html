The route from Maidstone to Folkestone

From Maidstone, your driver normally joins the M20 at Junction 5, 6 or 7 on the edge of town, then follows the M20 east past Ashford to Junction 11a for the Eurotunnel terminal at Cheriton, or on to Junctions 12 and 13 for Folkestone town and harbour.

The M20 is usually free-flowing east of Maidstone, but cross-Channel traffic management (Operation Brock) can be in place at busy times, particularly in summer and around holidays. Your driver checks the latest before setting off.

In normal traffic the 38-mile journey from Maidstone to Folkestone & Eurotunnel takes 50–70 mins.

Pickup in Maidstone

Maidstone is Kent's county town, and its position on the M20 makes it quick to reach both the Channel ports to the east and, via the M26 and M25, the London airports to the west.

We collect from ME14, ME15, ME16 and ME17, including the town centre around Week Street and Fremlin Walk, the streets near Maidstone East and West stations, Allington, Bearsted, Loose and Coxheath. Tell us your postcode and we come to your door.

At Folkestone & Eurotunnel

Folkestone has the Eurotunnel Le Shuttle terminal at Cheriton, Folkestone Central and West stations, and the regenerated Harbour Arm. Le Shuttle carries vehicles rather than foot passengers, so tell us exactly where in Folkestone you are going.

There is no airport-style drop-off fee in Folkestone. Your driver takes you to the exact address, station or terminal building you give us.

If you are meeting a Le Shuttle crossing, check the operator's own check-in window before you choose a pickup time.

Folkestone to Maidstone taxi

Coming home from Folkestone? We collect you from Folkestone & Eurotunnel and drive you back to your door in Maidstone. For return journeys, we can collect you from any Folkestone address, station or the Eurotunnel terminal at an agreed time. You can book the return leg at the same time as your outbound journey.

Maidstone to Folkestone taxi: frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Maidstone to Folkestone & Eurotunnel?

A fixed-price taxi from Maidstone to Folkestone & Eurotunnel costs from £107 for a saloon carrying one to four passengers. An estate or MPV is about £128, a minibus about £150 and an executive saloon about £161. The fare is agreed when you book and does not change with traffic.

How long does a taxi from Maidstone to Folkestone take?

The journey is about 38 miles by road and normally takes 50–70 mins. Allow more time at weekday peaks (roughly 7–9:30am and 4–7pm), on Friday afternoons and at the start of school holidays.

Which route do you take from Maidstone to Folkestone?

From Maidstone, your driver normally joins the M20 at Junction 5, 6 or 7 on the edge of town, then follows the M20 east past Ashford to Junction 11a for the Eurotunnel terminal at Cheriton, or on to Junctions 12 and 13 for Folkestone town and harbour. Your driver checks live traffic before setting off and can change route if there is an incident.

When should I leave Maidstone for Folkestone?

If you are meeting a Le Shuttle crossing, check the operator's own check-in window before you choose a pickup time. With a journey of 50–70 mins from Maidstone, we will suggest a pickup time when you book.

Will the driver help with luggage at Folkestone?

Yes. Luggage help is included, and your driver takes you to the terminal or address you give us rather than a general drop-off point.

Are there any extra charges on the way to Folkestone?

There is no airport-style drop-off fee in Folkestone. Your driver takes you to the exact address, station or terminal building you give us.

Can you collect me from home or from Maidstone East station in Maidstone?

Yes. We collect from any address in ME14, ME15, ME16 and ME17, or from Maidstone East station or a landmark you name when booking. Give us the full postcode so your driver can find rural addresses easily.

Can you take me back from Folkestone to Maidstone?

Yes. We run the same fixed-price service from Folkestone & Eurotunnel back to any address in Maidstone. Book both legs together and we will confirm both fares.

How many passengers and cases fit?

A saloon takes up to four passengers with standard luggage, an estate or MPV five or six, and a minibus seven or eight. Tell us how many large cases you have so we send a vehicle with enough room.

Do you run early-morning taxis from Maidstone?

Yes, we operate 24 hours a day, seven days a week. For pickups before 6am please book at least 48 hours ahead so your driver is confirmed well before the day.

Are your drivers licensed?

Yes. All our drivers are licensed by Tunbridge Wells Borough Council, DBS-checked and professionally trained, and we are a licensed private hire operator in Kent.
