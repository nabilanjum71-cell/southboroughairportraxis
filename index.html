FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About
Airport Taxis from Southborough

How much is a taxi from Southborough to Gatwick Airport?+
A fixed-price taxi from Southborough to Gatwick Airport costs from £72 for a saloon (1–4 passengers). The journey takes about 40–55 minutes. The price includes door-to-door pickup, flight tracking and meet & greet for arrivals, and it does not change if your flight is delayed.
How much is a taxi from Tunbridge Wells to Gatwick Airport?+
A fixed-price taxi from Tunbridge Wells to Gatwick costs from £78 for a saloon (1–4 passengers), and the journey takes about 40–55 minutes. MPVs, minibuses and executive cars are also available. Call 07808 065494 for your exact fare.
How much is a taxi from Southborough to Heathrow Airport?+
A fixed-price taxi from Southborough to Heathrow costs from £95 for a saloon (1–4 passengers). The journey normally takes around 65–85 minutes via the A21 and the M25. All four terminals (2, 3, 4 and 5) are covered.
Do you charge extra if my flight is delayed?+
No. We track your flight in real time and adjust your pickup automatically. The price you booked is the price you pay, whatever time you land.
Which airport is closest to Southborough and Tunbridge Wells?+
Gatwick is the closest airport to Southborough and Tunbridge Wells, about 25 miles by road and 40–55 minutes by taxi. London City and Heathrow come next, then Stansted and Luton north of the Thames.
What vehicles do you have?+
Saloon cars for 1–4 passengers, estates and MPVs for 5–6, minibuses for 7–8, and executive saloons for 1–4 passengers who want extra comfort.
Do you provide meet and greet at the airport?+
Yes. For airport arrivals your driver meets you inside the terminal with a name board, and there is no waiting charge for the first 60 minutes after landing.
Which towns do you cover?+
We collect from Southborough, Tunbridge Wells, Tonbridge, Sevenoaks, Paddock Wood, Maidstone, Crowborough, Edenbridge, East Grinstead, Westerham, Hartfield, Hawkhurst and the villages around them.
How far in advance should I book?+
We recommend at least 24 hours for airport transfers and 48 hours for pickups before 6am. Last-minute bookings are sometimes possible, so call 07808 065494 to check.
Are your drivers licensed and DBS checked?+
Yes. All our drivers are licensed by Tunbridge Wells Borough Council, DBS-checked and professionally trained, and we are a licensed private hire operator in Kent.
Do you accept card payments?+
Yes, cash and card are accepted. Please mention card payment when booking. Bank transfer is accepted for advance bookings.
Can I get a child seat?+
Yes, child seats are available on request at no extra charge. Tell us your child's age and weight when booking so we fit the right seat.
