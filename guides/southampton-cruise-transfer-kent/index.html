Getting to Southampton Cruise Terminal from Kent

By the Southborough Airport Taxis team · Published 6 May 2025 · Updated 15 November 2026

Short answer

Southampton is roughly 100 miles from Southborough and Tunbridge Wells. A taxi takes about two to two and a half hours via the M25 clockwise and the M3, with fixed fares from £120 for a saloon. Plan your pickup around the boarding window on your cruise documents, plus a traffic buffer.

The route

From West Kent your driver heads to Junction 5 or 6 of the M25, travels clockwise to Junction 12, then takes the M3 south-west to its end at Junction 14. From there the A33 leads into the city and down to the docks.

Which terminal?

Southampton has several cruise terminals across the Eastern and Western Docks, including Ocean, Mayflower, City, QEII and Horizon. Your cruise documents name the terminal. Give it to us when you book and your driver goes straight there.

Timing your pickup

Cruise lines issue boarding windows, and arriving very early does not usually get you on board sooner. Work back from the start of your window, allow two and a half hours for the drive, and add a buffer for the M25. We will suggest a pickup time when you book.

Luggage

Cruise luggage is often bulky. A saloon suits two people with two large cases each; for four people with cruise luggage, an estate or MPV is the better choice. At the terminal, porters take your cases from the drop-off.
