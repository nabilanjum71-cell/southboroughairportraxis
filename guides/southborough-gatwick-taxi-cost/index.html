How Much Is a Taxi from Southborough to Gatwick?

By the Southborough Airport Taxis team · Published 10 March 2025 · Updated 15 November 2026

Short answer

A pre-booked fixed-price taxi from Southborough to Gatwick costs from £72 for a saloon carrying up to four passengers, and from £78 from Tunbridge Wells. The journey takes about 40–55 minutes. Larger vehicles cost more: an MPV is about 1.2× the saloon fare, a minibus about 1.4× and an executive car about 1.5×.

What the fare includes

Our Gatwick fares are fixed when you book. They include door-to-door pickup anywhere in TN4, flight tracking for arrivals, meet & greet inside the terminal, and up to 60 minutes of free waiting after landing. Traffic on the M25 or a delayed flight does not change the price.

Gatwick sets its own terminal drop-off charge, which is separate from the taxi fare and can change. Ask us when you book how it is handled, so the total cost is clear before you travel.

Fares by vehicle

From Southborough, a saloon (1–4 passengers) is from £72, an estate or MPV (5–6 passengers) about £86, a minibus (7–8 passengers) about £101 and an executive saloon about £108. From Tunbridge Wells, add roughly £6 to each.

If you are travelling with several large cases, golf bags or a pushchair, tell us when you book. A saloon fits four people with standard luggage; an estate or MPV is the safer choice for families with bulky bags.

How to compare quotes fairly

When you compare airport taxis, check whether the quote is fixed or metered, whether flight delays cost extra, whether waiting time after landing is included, and whether the driver is licensed by the local council. A lower headline price can end up more expensive if waiting time or parking is added on the day.

App-based ride-hailing prices move with demand, which can make early-morning and bank-holiday trips much dearer than expected. A pre-booked fixed fare removes that uncertainty.

Taxi or train to Gatwick?

There is no direct train from Southborough or Tunbridge Wells to Gatwick; most rail journeys involve at least one change. For a solo traveller with light luggage on a daytime flight, rail can be cheaper. For couples, families, early departures or heavy luggage, a door-to-door taxi is usually simpler and often comparable in total cost.
