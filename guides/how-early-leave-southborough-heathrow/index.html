How Early Should You Leave Southborough for a Heathrow Flight?

By the Southborough Airport Taxis team · Published 18 March 2025 · Updated 15 November 2026

Short answer

For a morning Heathrow flight, plan to arrive at the airport two hours before a short-haul departure or three hours before long-haul, then add the 65–85 minute drive from Southborough and a 30-minute traffic buffer. For a 9am long-haul flight, that means a pickup at around 4:30am.

Work backwards from your flight

Start with your departure time, subtract the time your airline wants you at the airport (usually two hours for short-haul and three for long-haul), then subtract the journey time and a buffer. From Southborough the drive to Heathrow normally takes 65–85 minutes via the A21 and the M25.

Our route pages include a pickup planning table for each town, so you can see suggested pickup times for 6am, 9am, midday and 5pm departures.

Why the M25 matters

From West Kent you join the M25 at Junction 5 near Sevenoaks and travel clockwise past the M23 and M3 junctions to Heathrow. The west side of the M25 between Junctions 10 and 15 is one of the busiest stretches of road in the country, and congestion builds quickly from about 6:30am on weekdays.

Very early departures are easier: a 4am pickup usually means a clear run. Friday afternoons, bank holiday weekends and the start of school holidays are the times to add the most margin.

Terminal matters too

Heathrow has four terminals: 2, 3, 4 and 5. Terminals 4 and 5 are reached from Junction 14 of the M25, while Terminals 2 and 3 are reached via the M4 spur. Tell us your terminal when you book so your driver takes the right approach.

Arriving back

For return flights we track your arrival and meet you inside the terminal. Long-haul arrivals can take an hour or more to clear immigration and baggage reclaim, and our first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free.
