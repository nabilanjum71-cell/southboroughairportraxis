About pickups in Sevenoaks

Sevenoaks sits right beside Junction 5 of the M25, so it has one of the shortest motorway approaches of any town we serve. That makes a real difference on early departures, when the ring road is at its quietest.

We collect from TN13, TN14 and TN15, including the High Street, the roads around Sevenoaks and Bat & Ball stations, Riverhead, Dunton Green, Kippington, Sevenoaks Weald and the edge of Knole Park. Give us your full postcode and your driver comes to your door.

Getting from Sevenoaks to the airports

For Gatwick, Heathrow, Luton and the Southampton cruise terminals your driver normally joins the M25 at Junction 5 on the edge of town, then follows the M25 clockwise.

For Stansted, London City, Tilbury and Harwich the route heads anticlockwise round the M25 towards the Dartford Crossing or the A2. For Folkestone and Dover your driver takes the M26 east from Junction 5 of the M25 and joins the M20, then heads east.

Sevenoaks airport taxi FAQs

Which airport is closest to Sevenoaks?

London City Airport is the closest airport to Sevenoaks, about 23 miles and 35–45 mins by taxi. Gatwick Airport is next at about 23 miles (35–45 mins).

How much is an airport taxi from Sevenoaks?

Fixed fares from Sevenoaks start at £100 to Gatwick, £123 to Heathrow, £118 to London City, £158 to Stansted and £176 to Luton, for a saloon carrying up to four passengers.

Do you charge for flight delays?

No. We track your flight and adjust your pickup automatically, and the first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free.

Often, yes. Call 07808 065494 and we will tell you what we can arrange. For early-morning pickups, booking at least 48 hours ahead is best.
