About pickups in Hawkhurst

Hawkhurst is a Weald town on the Kent and East Sussex border, at the junction of the A229 and A268. It is the furthest south-east of the towns we serve, so we plan pickup times with a little more margin.

We collect from TN18, including the Moor, Rye Road, Highgate, Gills Green and nearby villages such as Sandhurst, Cranbrook, Flimwell and Hawkhurst Moor. Give us your full postcode when you book and your driver comes to your door.

Getting from Hawkhurst to the airports

For Gatwick, Heathrow, Luton and the Southampton cruise terminals your driver normally takes the A21 north through Flimwell and Lamberhurst, past Tonbridge to Junction 5 of the M25, then follows the M25 clockwise.

For Stansted, London City, Tilbury and Harwich the route heads anticlockwise round the M25 towards the Dartford Crossing or the A2. For Folkestone and Dover your driver heads east across the Weald via Cranbrook and the A262 and A28 to Ashford, joining the M20 at Junction 9, then heads east.
