About pickups in Westerham

Westerham is a small historic town on the A25 between Sevenoaks and Oxted, with the M25 just to the north. It has no station of its own, which makes a pre-booked taxi the easiest way to the airports.

We collect from TN16, including the Green, Quebec Square, the roads towards Chartwell, Brasted, Crockham Hill and Biggin Hill. Give us your full postcode when you book and your driver comes to your door.

Getting from Westerham to the airports

For Gatwick, Heathrow, Luton and the Southampton cruise terminals your driver normally takes the A25 west to Junction 6 of the M25 at Godstone, then follows the M25 clockwise.

For Stansted, London City, Tilbury and Harwich the route heads anticlockwise round the M25 towards the Dartford Crossing or the A2. For Folkestone and Dover your driver takes the A25 east to Junction 5 of the M25 and the M26 onto the M20, then heads east.

Westerham airport taxi FAQs

Which airport is closest to Westerham?

Gatwick Airport is the closest airport to Westerham, about 17 miles and 30–40 mins by taxi. London City Airport is next at about 22 miles (35–45 mins).

How much is an airport taxi from Westerham?

Fixed fares from Westerham start at £110 to Gatwick, £133 to Heathrow, £128 to London City, £168 to Stansted and £186 to Luton, for a saloon carrying up to four passengers.

Do you pick up from anywhere in Westerham?

Do you charge for flight delays?

No. We track your flight and adjust your pickup automatically, and the first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free.

Can I book an airport taxi from Westerham at short notice?

Often, yes. Call 07808 065494 and we will tell you what we can arrange. For early-morning pickups, booking at least 48 hours ahead is best.
