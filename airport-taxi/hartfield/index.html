About pickups in Hartfield

Hartfield is a village on the northern edge of Ashdown Forest in East Sussex, with no railway station. A pre-booked taxi is the practical way to an airport or port, especially with luggage or an early start.

We collect from TN7, including the High Street, Coleman's Hatch, Withyham, Forest Row and the lanes across Ashdown Forest. Give us your full postcode, and any directions for rural addresses, when you book.

Getting from Hartfield to the airports

For Gatwick, Heathrow, Luton and the Southampton cruise terminals your driver normally heads to Forest Row and takes the A22 north through East Grinstead to Junction 6 of the M25, then follows the M25 clockwise. For Gatwick there is also a shorter cross-country route: your driver heads to Forest Row and East Grinstead, then takes the A264 through Copthorne to the M23 at Junction 10 for Gatwick.

For Stansted, London City, Tilbury and Harwich the route heads anticlockwise round the M25 towards the Dartford Crossing or the A2. For Folkestone and Dover your driver heads east on the A264 to Tunbridge Wells, then north through Tonbridge on the A26 to join the M20 near West Malling, then heads east.

Hartfield airport taxi FAQs

Which airport is closest to Hartfield?

Gatwick Airport is the closest airport to Hartfield, about 16 miles and 25–35 mins by taxi. London City Airport is next at about 38 miles (50–70 mins).

How much is an airport taxi from Hartfield?

Fixed fares from Hartfield start at £102 to Gatwick, £125 to Heathrow, £120 to London City, £160 to Stansted and £178 to Luton, for a saloon carrying up to four passengers.

Do you charge for flight delays?

No. We track your flight and adjust your pickup automatically, and the first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free.
