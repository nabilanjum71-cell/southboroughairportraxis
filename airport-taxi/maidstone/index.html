About pickups in Maidstone

Maidstone is Kent's county town, and its position on the M20 makes it quick to reach both the Channel ports to the east and, via the M26 and M25, the London airports to the west.

We collect from ME14, ME15, ME16 and ME17, including the town centre around Week Street and Fremlin Walk, the streets near Maidstone East and West stations, Allington, Bearsted, Loose and Coxheath. Tell us your postcode and we come to your door.

Getting from Maidstone to the airports

For Gatwick, Heathrow, Luton and the Southampton cruise terminals your driver normally takes the M20 west and the M26 to Junction 5 of the M25, then follows the M25 clockwise.

For Stansted, London City, Tilbury and Harwich the route heads anticlockwise round the M25 towards the Dartford Crossing or the A2. For Folkestone and Dover your driver joins the M20 at Junction 5, 6 or 7 on the edge of town, then heads east.

Maidstone airport taxi FAQs

Which airport is closest to Maidstone?

London City Airport is the closest airport to Maidstone, about 35 miles and 50–65 mins by taxi. Gatwick Airport is next at about 41 miles (50–65 mins).

How much is an airport taxi from Maidstone?

Fixed fares from Maidstone start at £114 to Gatwick, £137 to Heathrow, £132 to London City, £172 to Stansted and £190 to Luton, for a saloon carrying up to four passengers.

Do you pick up from anywhere in Maidstone?

We collect from ME14, ME15, ME16 and ME17, including the town centre around Week Street and Fremlin Walk, the streets near Maidstone East and West stations, Allington, Bearsted, Loose and Coxheath. Tell us your postcode and we come to your door.

Do you charge for flight delays?

No. We track your flight and adjust your pickup automatically, and the first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free.

Can I book an airport taxi from Maidstone at short notice?

Often, yes. Call 07808 065494 and we will tell you what we can arrange. For early-morning pickups, booking at least 48 hours ahead is best.
