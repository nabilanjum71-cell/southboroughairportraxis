About pickups in Paddock Wood

Paddock Wood is a small Weald town surrounded by orchards and old hop country. It is a little further from the motorways than Tonbridge, so a fixed door-to-door fare removes the hassle of getting to a bigger station first.

We collect from TN12, including Commercial Road, the roads around Paddock Wood station and the surrounding villages of Five Oak Green, Brenchley, Matfield, Horsmonden and East Peckham. Give us your full postcode so your driver can find rural addresses without delay.

Getting from Paddock Wood to the airports

For Gatwick, Heathrow, Luton and the Southampton cruise terminals your driver normally heads west on the B2017 into Tonbridge and joins the A21 north to Junction 5 of the M25, then follows the M25 clockwise.

For Stansted, London City, Tilbury and Harwich the route heads anticlockwise round the M25 towards the Dartford Crossing or the A2. For Folkestone and Dover your driver heads north on the A228 to join the M20 at Junction 4 near West Malling, then heads east.
