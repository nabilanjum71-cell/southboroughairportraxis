About pickups in Crowborough

Crowborough sits high on the edge of Ashdown Forest in East Sussex. It is closer to Gatwick than many people expect, because the cross-country route through East Grinstead avoids the M25 entirely.

We collect from TN6, including Crowborough Cross, the High Street, Jarvis Brook and the roads around Crowborough station, plus nearby villages such as Rotherfield, Boarshead and Mark Cross. Give us your full postcode when you book and your driver comes to your door.

Getting from Crowborough to the airports

For Gatwick, Heathrow, Luton and the Southampton cruise terminals your driver normally heads north on the A26 to Tunbridge Wells and takes the A21 north to Junction 5 of the M25, then follows the M25 clockwise. For Gatwick there is also a shorter cross-country route: your driver heads west to the A22 and through East Grinstead, then takes the A264 through Copthorne to the M23 at Junction 10 for Gatwick.

For Stansted, London City, Tilbury and Harwich the route heads anticlockwise round the M25 towards the Dartford Crossing or the A2. For Folkestone and Dover your driver heads north through Tunbridge Wells and Tonbridge on the A26 to join the M20 near West Malling, then heads east.

Crowborough airport taxi FAQs

Which airport is closest to Crowborough?

Gatwick Airport is the closest airport to Crowborough, about 20 miles and 30–40 mins by taxi. London City Airport is next at about 42 miles (55–75 mins).

How much is an airport taxi from Crowborough?

Fixed fares from Crowborough start at £94 to Gatwick, £117 to Heathrow, £112 to London City, £152 to Stansted and £170 to Luton, for a saloon carrying up to four passengers.

Do you charge for flight delays?

No. We track your flight and adjust your pickup automatically, and the first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free.
