About pickups in Tunbridge Wells

Royal Tunbridge Wells is the main town in this corner of Kent and the most common starting point for the airport runs we make, from town-centre flats near the Pantiles to family homes in Pembury, Rusthall and Langton Green.

We cover TN1, TN2, TN3 and TN4, including the Pantiles, Mount Pleasant, Calverley Road, St John's, Sherwood, Pembury, Rusthall, Langton Green and the roads around Tunbridge Wells and High Brooms stations. Give us your full address and your driver collects you from the door.

Getting from Tunbridge Wells to the airports

For Gatwick, Heathrow, Luton and the Southampton cruise terminals your driver normally heads north on the A21 past Tonbridge to Junction 5 of the M25 near Sevenoaks, then follows the M25 clockwise.

For Stansted, London City, Tilbury and Harwich the route heads anticlockwise round the M25 towards the Dartford Crossing or the A2. For Folkestone and Dover your driver heads north through Tonbridge and along the A26 and A228 to join the M20 near West Malling, then heads east.

Tunbridge Wells airport taxi FAQs

Which airport is closest to Tunbridge Wells?

Gatwick Airport is the closest airport to Tunbridge Wells, about 25 miles and 40–55 mins by taxi. London City Airport is next at about 37 miles (50–65 mins).

How much is an airport taxi from Tunbridge Wells?

Fixed fares from Tunbridge Wells start at £78 to Gatwick, £101 to Heathrow, £96 to London City, £136 to Stansted and £154 to Luton, for a saloon carrying up to four passengers.
