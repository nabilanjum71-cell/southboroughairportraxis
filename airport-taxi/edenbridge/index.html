About pickups in Edenbridge

Edenbridge is a small market town in the Eden valley, close to the Surrey border. Its rail links to the airports involve changes, so a door-to-door taxi is often the simplest way to reach Gatwick or Heathrow.

We collect from TN8, including the High Street, the roads around Edenbridge and Edenbridge Town stations, Marlpit Hill, Hever, Cowden and Four Elms. Give us your full postcode when you book and your driver comes to your door.

Getting from Edenbridge to the airports

For Gatwick, Heathrow, Luton and the Southampton cruise terminals your driver normally heads north on the B2026 to Westerham and joins the M25 at Junction 6 near Godstone, then follows the M25 clockwise. For Gatwick there is also a shorter cross-country route: your driver heads west through Lingfield to the A22 and takes the A264 through Copthorne to the M23 at Junction 10 for Gatwick.

For Stansted, London City, Tilbury and Harwich the route heads anticlockwise round the M25 towards the Dartford Crossing or the A2. For Folkestone and Dover your driver heads north to the A25 and Junction 5 of the M25, then takes the M26 east onto the M20, then heads east.

Edenbridge airport taxi FAQs

Which airport is closest to Edenbridge?

Gatwick Airport is the closest airport to Edenbridge, about 14 miles and 25–35 mins by taxi. London City Airport is next at about 29 miles (40–55 mins).

How much is an airport taxi from Edenbridge?

Fixed fares from Edenbridge start at £104 to Gatwick, £127 to Heathrow, £122 to London City, £162 to Stansted and £180 to Luton, for a saloon carrying up to four passengers.

Do you pick up from anywhere in Edenbridge?

We collect from TN8, including the High Street, the roads around Edenbridge and Edenbridge Town stations, Marlpit Hill, Hever, Cowden and Four Elms. Give us your full postcode when you book and your driver comes to your door.

Do you charge for flight delays?

No. We track your flight and adjust your pickup automatically, and the first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free.

Can I book an airport taxi from Edenbridge at short notice?

Often, yes. Call 07808 065494 and we will tell you what we can arrange. For early-morning pickups, booking at least 48 hours ahead is best.
