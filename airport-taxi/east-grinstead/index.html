About pickups in East Grinstead

East Grinstead is a West Sussex town where Kent, Surrey and Sussex meet. Gatwick is on its doorstep via the A264, while the airports north of the Thames are a long way round, which is where a fixed fare really helps.

We collect from RH19, including the High Street, the roads around East Grinstead station, Saint Hill Road, Felbridge, Ashurst Wood and Forest Row. Give us your full postcode when you book and your driver comes to your door.

Getting from East Grinstead to the airports

For Gatwick, Heathrow, Luton and the Southampton cruise terminals your driver normally takes the A22 north through Felbridge and Blindley Heath to Junction 6 of the M25 at Godstone, then follows the M25 clockwise. For Gatwick there is also a shorter cross-country route: your driver takes the A264 west through Felbridge and Copthorne to the M23 at Junction 10 for Gatwick.

For Stansted, London City, Tilbury and Harwich the route heads anticlockwise round the M25 towards the Dartford Crossing or the A2. For Folkestone and Dover your driver takes the A22 north to Junction 6 of the M25, follows the M25 to Junction 5 and takes the M26 east onto the M20, then heads east.

East Grinstead airport taxi FAQs

Which airport is closest to East Grinstead?

Gatwick Airport is the closest airport to East Grinstead, about 10 miles and 20–30 mins by taxi. London City Airport is next at about 36 miles (50–65 mins).

How much is an airport taxi from East Grinstead?

Fixed fares from East Grinstead start at £116 to Gatwick, £139 to Heathrow, £134 to London City, £174 to Stansted and £192 to Luton, for a saloon carrying up to four passengers.

Do you pick up from anywhere in East Grinstead?

We collect from RH19, including the High Street, the roads around East Grinstead station, Saint Hill Road, Felbridge, Ashurst Wood and Forest Row. Give us your full postcode when you book and your driver comes to your door.

Do you charge for flight delays?

No. We track your flight and adjust your pickup automatically, and the first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free.

Can I book an airport taxi from East Grinstead at short notice?

Often, yes. Call 07808 065494 and we will tell you what we can arrange. For early-morning pickups, booking at least 48 hours ahead is best.
