Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Southborough Airport Taxis ("we", "us"), operated by C Line Cars, is based in Southborough, Tunbridge Wells, Kent TN4. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal data when you use our website or book our services.

What data we collect

  • Name, phone number and email address
  • Pickup and destination addresses
  • Journey details (date, time, vehicle type)
  • Flight number where provided
  • Messages you send us by WhatsApp, phone or email

How we use your data

  • To confirm and fulfil your booking
  • To contact you about your journey
  • To track your flight where a number is provided

How the booking form works

The booking form on our website does not store your details on our web server. When you submit it, it opens WhatsApp on your device with your booking details filled in, and nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that message.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data or share it for marketing. We share only what is needed with the driver carrying out your booking.

Data retention

We keep booking records for up to two years for accounting and legal purposes. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your rights

  • Access to your personal data
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure
  • Restriction of processing
  • Data portability

Contact us

For any privacy query, email clinetaxi@gmail.com or call 07808 065494.
