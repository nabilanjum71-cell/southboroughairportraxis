About Southborough Airport Taxis

In brief

Southborough Airport Taxis is a licensed private hire operator based in Southborough, Kent (TN4), trading as C Line Cars. We specialise in fixed-price airport and seaport transfers from Southborough, Tunbridge Wells and 10 other towns in West Kent and East and West Sussex, 24 hours a day.

What we do

We focus on one thing: getting people from West Kent to their flight, ferry or cruise on time, at a price agreed in advance. We cover all five London airports — Gatwick, Heathrow, London City, Stansted and Luton — and the ports of Dover, Folkestone, Tilbury, Southampton and Harwich.

Licensing and safety

Our drivers are licensed by Tunbridge Wells Borough Council, DBS-checked and professionally trained. Vehicles are licensed for private hire, insured for hire and reward, and kept clean and well maintained.

How our pricing works

Every journey has a fixed fare, agreed when you book. Traffic does not change it, and neither do flight delays: we track flights and adjust pickups automatically, and the first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free.
