About London Tilbury Cruise Terminal

Tilbury's London International Cruise Terminal sits on the Thames riverside and is used by several cruise lines, including Ambassador Cruise Line. Check your cruise documents for your boarding window.

Your driver takes you to the cruise terminal drop-off and helps with luggage. The route uses the Dartford Crossing, which has a road-user charge, so ask us how it is handled on your fare.

Cruise lines allocate boarding times; arrive within your window rather than early, and we will plan the pickup around it.

The route to Tilbury

From Tunbridge Wells, your driver normally heads north on the A21 past Tonbridge to Junction 5 of the M25 near Sevenoaks, then follows the M25 anticlockwise over the Dartford Crossing, leaves at Junction 30 for the A13 east and follows the A1089 down to Tilbury and the cruise terminal.

The Dartford Crossing is the main pinch-point: northbound queues can build quickly at peak times and after incidents, and the crossing has a road-user charge (Dart Charge). Ask us how the charge is handled on your fixed fare.
