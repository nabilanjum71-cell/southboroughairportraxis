About Stansted Airport

Stansted has a single passenger terminal, so there is no terminal to choose. Your driver drops you at the terminal forecourt and helps with your luggage.

Stansted charges for its terminal drop-off zone (£10 for a short stop at the time of writing). The route also uses the Dartford Crossing, which has a road-user charge. Ask us at booking how both are handled on your fare.

Most airlines recommend arriving two hours before a short-haul flight; Stansted security can be busy at peak times, so allow extra time in school holidays.

The route to Stansted

From Tunbridge Wells, your driver normally heads north on the A21 past Tonbridge to Junction 5 of the M25 near Sevenoaks, then follows the M25 anticlockwise over the Dartford Crossing, continues north to Junction 27 and takes the M11 to Junction 8 for the airport.

The Dartford Crossing is the main pinch-point: northbound queues can build quickly at peak times and after incidents, and the crossing has a road-user charge (Dart Charge). Ask us how the charge is handled on your fixed fare.

Stansted taxi FAQs

How much is a taxi to Stansted Airport from Kent?

Fixed fares to Stansted Airport start at £130 from Southborough and £136 from Tunbridge Wells for a saloon carrying up to four passengers. The table on this page lists the fare from every town we cover.

Which terminal will I be dropped at?

Stansted has a single passenger terminal, so there is no terminal to choose. Your driver drops you at the terminal forecourt and helps with your luggage.

Are there extra charges?

Stansted charges for its terminal drop-off zone (£10 for a short stop at the time of writing). The route also uses the Dartford Crossing, which has a road-user charge. Ask us at booking how both are handled on your fare.

Do you meet arriving flights at Stansted?

For arrivals, your driver tracks your flight and meets you inside the terminal, so a late landing does not change your fare.
