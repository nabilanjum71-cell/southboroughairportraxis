About Luton Airport

Luton has a single passenger terminal. Your driver uses the terminal drop-off area and helps with your luggage.

Luton charges for using its terminal drop-off zone. The fee is set by the airport and can change, so ask us at booking how it is handled on your fare.

Most airlines recommend arriving two hours before a short-haul flight.

The route to Luton

From Tunbridge Wells, your driver normally heads north on the A21 past Tonbridge to Junction 5 of the M25 near Sevenoaks, then follows the M25 clockwise round the west of London to Junction 21 and takes the M1 north to Junction 10 for the airport.

The west and north-west sides of the M25 up to the M1 are the busiest part of the journey, so we plan extra time for weekday morning and Friday departures.

Luton taxi FAQs

How much is a taxi to Luton Airport from Kent?

Fixed fares to Luton Airport start at £148 from Southborough and £154 from Tunbridge Wells for a saloon carrying up to four passengers. The table on this page lists the fare from every town we cover.

How long does it take to get to Luton from Tunbridge Wells?

About 1 hr 35 – 2 hr 5 in normal traffic for the 75-mile journey. The west and north-west sides of the M25 up to the M1 are the busiest part of the journey, so we plan extra time for weekday morning and Friday departures.

Which terminal will I be dropped at?

Luton has a single passenger terminal. Your driver uses the terminal drop-off area and helps with your luggage.

Are there extra charges?

Luton charges for using its terminal drop-off zone. The fee is set by the airport and can change, so ask us at booking how it is handled on your fare.

Do you meet arriving flights at Luton?

For arrivals, your driver tracks your flight and meets you inside the terminal, so a late landing does not change your fare.
